# S14 — very long chapter timing (Psalm 119) — 2026-11-20 (Claude Code)

Measured on the iPhone 17 Pro simulator (and on the Mac, below) (Apple Silicon host, Debug build) with temporary timing around `IOSChapterTextView` (render → assign `attributedText` → `ensureLayout`), opened by `berean-native-dev://verse/PSA/119?text=kjva`; instrumentation removed afterwards.

| Chapter | Characters | Build | Assign | Layout | Total |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| KJV Psalm 119 (3 runs) | 13,965 | 7.8–10.0 ms | 1.2–4.1 ms | 7.8–11.3 ms | ≈ 17–25 ms |
| KJV Genesis 1 (3 runs) | 4,203 | 1.9–2.1 ms | 0.9–2.1 ms | 1.7–1.9 ms | ≈ 5 ms |

- Whole-chapter TextKit rendering is fast enough: the longest chapter costs about one or two frames once, on open. No windowing needed.

- Caveat: a physical iPhone will be slower than the simulator — re-measure on device.
- Side finding: a `PSA/119` link without `text=` resolves in the current text; on a Brenton LXX tab that is LXX Psalm 119 (= KJV Psalm 120, 7 verses) — correct LXX numbering, worth knowing when testing.
