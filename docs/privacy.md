# Privacy Policy — Berean Bible Study

**Last updated: May 2026**

## Overview

Berean Bible Study does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data. The app is designed to work entirely offline with all data stored locally on your device.

## Data Collection

Berean Bible Study collects **no personal information**. Specifically:

- No account or sign-in is required
- No analytics or usage data are collected
- No crash reports are sent to any server
- No advertising identifiers are used
- No location data is accessed

## Local Storage

All data created in the app — including notes, highlights, bookmarks, and settings — is stored locally on your Mac. This data never leaves your device unless you manually sync it using your own tools (e.g. iCloud, Dropbox).

## Network Requests

The app may make outbound network requests in the following limited cases:

- **YouTube**: If you use the YouTube study tab, video playback is handled through YouTube's standard web interface. YouTube's own privacy policy applies to that content.
- **App updates**: The app may check GitHub Releases for available updates. No personal information is included in these requests.

No personal information is transmitted in any network request made by this app.

## Third-Party Content

All Bible text databases bundled with the app are sourced from public domain texts. No third-party tracking or analytics SDKs are included.
